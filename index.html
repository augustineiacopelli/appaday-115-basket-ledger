Scan a Receipt

Take a photo of a receipt and Claude will read off the store, date, and each item's price for you to review before adding. Uses your own Anthropic API key, added in Settings. Nothing is sent until you tap Extract Items.

    Log a Price

    Enter what you bought, what it cost, and where. Prices are grouped by item and store using a lowercase match, so "Milk" and "milk" count as the same item.

    Price Over Time

    Pick an item to see how its price has moved across every store where you logged it.

    Store Averages

    Average price per logged entry at each store, ranked lowest to highest. This blends every item together, so it is a general sense of where you shop cheaper, not a like-for-like comparison.
